Frequently Asked Questions

What is Robomatonix?

Robomatonix is an organization dedicated to empowering students to explore the exciting world of robotics and technology.

What age groups or classes are your programs designed for?

We offer customized programs for students from Class 1 to Class 12, as well as college students. Our activities are tailored to suit different age groups, with separate modules designed for junior, senior, and college-level learners.

Where do your workshops take place?

We conduct workshops and bootcamps in schools, colleges, and educational events across India. We also offer services to set up permanent STEM-Robotics & AI labs in schools nationwide.

What is included in your 3D Pen Bootcamp?

Students learn how to use 3D pens, create basic and advanced 3D models, and understand real-world applications of 3D technology.

What do students learn in the Robotics Workshop?

Students explore sensors, Arduino, IoT, and basic coding. They build hands-on robotics projects to understand real-world technology applications.

Can students fly real drones?

Yes! In our Drone Pilot Training, students use drone simulators and also fly real drones to understand both control and construction.

How can a school invite Robomatonix for a workshop?

Schools can contact us directly via our website www.robomatonix.com, email us at robomatonix@gmail.com, or call 7062847650.

Are these workshops one-time or recurring?

We offer both one-time bootcamps and long-term STEM curriculum partnerships depending on school needs.

Do students get certificates?

Yes, students receive participation certificates after completing our workshops or bootcamps.

What makes Robomatonix workshops unique?

Our programs combine practical experiments, real-world applications, and engaging activities. We also customize content based on student age groups and school goals.
