About

Robomatonix is an EdTech startup company, dedicated to transforming STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics) education through hands-on, interactive learning experiences. Founded by Swetansh Bansal, the company bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application by offering engaging programs such as Coding classes, Robotics workshop, 3D pen bootcamps, Drone training, Champions league, Game development, App development, etc. Our mission is to prepare students for future success in a technology-driven world.

Our Programs

Robotics/STEM Lab

We sets up STEM-Robotics labs in schools, equipping students with tools for hands-on learning in science and technology.


Robomatonix Champions League (RCL)

RCL is a school level competition featuring exciting events like Robo-War, Robo-Soccer, Black Line Follower, and Robo-Racer, where students showcase their robotics skills.

3D Pen Bootcamp

Empower students to unleash their creativity by mastering the use of 3D pens to create models and designs & apply their skills in creative projects.


2D Animation Workshop

Learn the art of 2D animation, from creating storyboards to designing characters and crafting smooth animations using cutting-edge software, fostering creativity and storytelling skills.

Drone Pilot Training

Students learn to control and fly drones, understanding drone construction and functionality.



Robotics / Coding Workshop

Students learn about sensors, Arduino, IoT devices, and coding languages. They create projects using sensors and Arduino, integrating their knowledge into practical applications.
